About us

The problem we solve

Charities struggle to reach volunteers, and people who want to volunteer struggle to learn which opportunities are available and when.

The solution we offer

Athar lists opportunities in a clear table, lets you sign up in one step, and shows the remaining seats and your hours.

Our values

  1. Clarity: every opportunity with its date, hours and seats.
  2. Simplicity: sign-up in as few steps as possible.
  3. Impact: we measure success by the hours contributed.
