The idea behind the platform

Many people want to volunteer but don't know where to start, and many charities need volunteers but can't reach them. Athar brings both sides together: it lists opportunities, lets you sign up in one step, and counts your volunteering hours.

Platform benefits

For the volunteer

  • Opportunities near their city
  • Quick sign-up with one click
  • A volunteering hours counter

For the charity

  • Reach more volunteers
  • Organize available seats

For the community

  • More services for those in need
  • A stronger culture of impact

How do you get started?

  1. Create an account from the New account page.
  2. Choose an opportunity from the table.
  3. Click Sign me up and watch your hours grow.
Who can volunteer?

Anyone who wants to give some of their time.
